# 🎨 Painty 0.2.3

### What's New

- 🌍 **Three New Languages** — Painty now speaks **Spanish**, **German** and **Italian**, in addition to English and French.
- 🗣️ **Pick Yours in Settings** — Choose your language from the drop-down in **Settings → Language**. It is saved for the next launch.

### 📦 Installation

Download `PaintySetup-0.2.3.exe` for a new installation.

Already have Painty installed? Update directly from **Settings → Check for updates**, or install the update when prompted at startup.
